> Independent teaching constructions, not observed model outputs or measured performance.

## Use case

Evaluate an alert assistant on the same100 cases:98 routine updates and2 urgent requests; missing an urgent request is the costly error.

## Mechanism

Name the affected decision and false-positive/false-negative costs; define relevant metrics, denominators and task-specific gates before inspecting candidate results.

## Bad example

```text
Approve an always-routine classifier for98% accuracy even though it misses both urgent requests.
```

## Good example

```text
Report urgent recall0/2 alongside unnecessary-alert count0/98; keep readiness blocked by the declared urgent-request policy and examine those two misses before changing model complexity.
```

## Why the change matters

Aggregate accuracy rewards the dominant class while concealing the failure the task most needs to prevent.

## Observable expectation

The evaluation records failure costs, per-metric counts and predeclared acceptance criteria, with the missed urgent cases visible.

## Limits

The numbers are a teaching fixture, not measured efficacy. Two urgent cases cannot establish deployment reliability; representative data and uncertainty remain necessary, and no universal threshold is implied.

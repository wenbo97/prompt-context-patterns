> These examples and illustrative results are independently authored teaching materials, not measured model results.

## Use case

An export request lacks audience, fields and destination. The teaching interface supports id/email/phone and local/shared targets; capability is not selection. Publishing sensitive full data creates consequences a revised prompt cannot recall.

## Mechanism

Inspect existing requirements/interfaces, separate consequential unknowns from safe routine decisions and present interpretations affecting scope/privacy/delivery. Resolve required choices before effects; reuse settled answers. Continue reversible preparation without making every minor detail an approval.

## Bad example

```text
Assume all records/fields and public destination, execute first and explain convenience afterward.
```
{: .example--bad}

## Good example

```text
Inspect the teaching request's existing scope. For missing audience/fields/location, explain ID-only internal/local versus broader shared interpretations and resolve the necessary choices. Until then prepare without leaking data, exporting or publishing. Record selected scope/assumptions and reuse answers, not convenient defaults as approval.
```
{: .example--good}

## Why the change matters

Hidden assumptions determine who receives what. Visible consequential interpretations allow correction, while fact discovery avoids needless questions.

## Observable expectation

The fixture's requirement names records, fields, recipient and target. ID-only internal choice does not include email/shared upload.

Keep selected versus assumed states separate without executing first. No real user data is used.

## Limits

Importance is task-specific; settled routine details need no interview. Design inference does not authorize guessed privacy/scope; low-risk choices follow the active contract.

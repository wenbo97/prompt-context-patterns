> Independent teaching constructions, not observed model outputs or measured performance.

## Use case

A draft becomes visually complex enough to benefit from a diagram.

## Mechanism

A visible task need makes an optional offer meaningful rather than speculative.

## Bad example

```text
Before reading the draft, ask about every visualization feature available.
```

## Good example

```text
Read the draft first. When the dependency section contains several interacting paths, offer one diagram with a sentence explaining what it clarifies. Continue plain-text work while the optional choice remains unanswered.
```

## Why the change matters

A visible task need makes an optional offer meaningful rather than speculative.

## Observable expectation

The offer names the relevant section and a specific reader benefit.

## Limits

Do not defer mandatory authorization or accessibility requirements as optional features.

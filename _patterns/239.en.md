> Independent teaching constructions, not observed model outputs or measured performance.

## Use case

Investigate an intermittent queue stall.

## Mechanism

Ask whether the artifact is known or must be found; preserve independent judgments for verdicts, and require falsifiable evidence exchange before investigative convergence.

## Bad example

```text
Run three isolated reviewers and count which theory receives two votes.
```

## Good example

```text
Assign distinct stall hypotheses, let investigators exchange discriminating observations, and verify the surviving prediction with a focused replay before convergence.
```

## Why the change matters

The procedure preserves the stated identity or evidence boundary instead of allowing a plausible shortcut to stand in for it.

## Observable expectation

Consensus is tied to disproof evidence rather than opinion count.

## Limits

Messaging or two-agent agreement alone does not establish a root cause; source host restrictions and efficacy claims are unverified.

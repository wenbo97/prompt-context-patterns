> These examples and illustrative results are independently authored teaching materials, not measured model results.

## Use case

An intermittent queue stall may be lock contention or remote retries, unlike reviewing a known diff. Both teaching theories fit surface delay and need discriminating observations, not votes after isolated essays.

## Mechanism

Determine known-artifact judgment versus causal discovery. Independent reports fit the former; the latter uses competing predictions, permitted evidence exchange and focused replay. Bound consensus to supported facts and retain indistinguishable explanations.

## Bad example

```text
Use three isolated theories, accept two votes and claim convergence without lock/call timing evidence.
```
{: .example--bad}

## Good example

```text
Specify discriminating predictions for contention/retries. Exchange permitted wait/call/timing records and attempt falsification, then replay the same inputs or a minimal probe. Unresolved ambiguity remains two hypotheses, not majority truth. Known-diff independent review uses report collection separately.
```
{: .example--good}

## Why the change matters

Investigation needs new evidence excluding explanations; review judges a fixed object. Collaboration should provide that value rather than replacing evidence with headcount.

## Observable expectation

Repeated remote calls without lock waits support a retry direction but still need replay; delay alone discriminates neither. Inspect predictions/observations, not agreement logs.

Both approaches can fail; no real incident is investigated.

## Limits

Discussion can anchor participants; retain initial hypotheses/counterevidence. Frozen host flags/versions or single-agent generalizations are not universal facts. Verify capabilities/cost and preserve tool authorization.

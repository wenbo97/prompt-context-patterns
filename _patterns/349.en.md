> Independent teaching constructions, not observed model outputs or measured performance.

## Use case

An authorized unattended analysis worker wedges inside a long tool call and stops emitting its configured task heartbeat.

## Mechanism

Before launch bind a supervisor to the current task/process group and the declared heartbeat deadline. Observe externally, use the authorized stop path on timeout, preserve logs and mark the task stalled for inspection rather than complete.

## Bad example

```text
Ask the same wedged worker to notice its missing heartbeat and stop itself; its blocked control path cannot execute the check.
```

## Good example

```text
The independent supervisor sees the same missing heartbeat, applies the preauthorized stop policy to that task instance, retains the trace and reports stalled instead of success.
```

## Why the change matters

A worker that has lost its control path cannot reliably enforce its own stop deadline; the separate supervisor can still act.

## Observable expectation

The record distinguishes heartbeat last seen, timeout decision, observed stop acknowledgement and quarantined task outcome; unresolved stop remains explicitly unknown.

## Limits

Heartbeat is liveness evidence, not proof of meaningful progress or compromise. Deadlines are task-specific; verify process identity and platform stop behavior, avoid affecting unrelated work, and do not infer new kill authority from a written pattern. This teaching case is not a tested runtime guarantee.

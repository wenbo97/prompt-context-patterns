> Independent teaching constructions, not observed model outputs or measured performance.

## Use case

Fix a failing coverage gate.

## Mechanism

Compare baseline thresholds, skipped/deleted tests, removed assertions, suppressions, stubs and new exceptions; require explained scoped exceptions.

## Bad example

```text
Lower the required coverage and skip a failing test, then report green.
```

## Good example

```text
Compare the gate against the branch baseline; keep its threshold, fix the uncovered behavior or record an owned expiring exception for review.
```

## Why the change matters

The contract names which evidence may be reused and which decision remains unresolved, so the result can be checked.

## Observable expectation

A successful verdict retains the originally accepted bar.

## Limits

A shallow diff guard can false-flag legitimate rewrites and miss semantic weakening; findings require review.

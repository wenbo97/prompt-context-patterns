> These examples and illustrative results are independently authored teaching materials, not measured model results.

## Use case

Audit overlapping deployment skills A/B and stale wrapper C. Overlap/too long labels do not guide implementation; dispositions need destinations, retained content and reasons.

## Mechanism

Inspect payloads/callers/evidence and give self-contained Keep/Improve/Retire/Merge rationales. Merges name target sections/content/conflicts/permissions; improvements name behavior; retirements name defects/verified replacements; keeps restate unique value. Mark unchecked currency and keep proposals distinct from deletion/install.

## Bad example

```text
A overlaps, B good, C old—labels complete the audit.
```
{: .example--bad}

## Good example

```text
Propose A rollback notes into B’s rollback section with duplicated and unique recovery checks identified. Retire C only after D is verified to cover need, retaining migration identity. Cite inspected paths, evidence, actions and unresolved contracts.
```
{: .example--good}

## Why the change matters

Self-contained reasons connect judgments to implementation without repeating investigation. Replacement/conflict details protect unique behavior.

## Observable expectation

Teaching entries have target/action/evidence; unverified D cannot retire C. Mtime-only unchanged keeps retain rationale/verification time. Usage frequency is not quality proof.

## Limits

Judgment remains and current tools need verification; frozen examples are not current facts. Permission/deletion changes follow actual authority, not the disposition label.

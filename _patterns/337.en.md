> Independent teaching constructions, not observed model outputs or measured performance.

## Use case

Review two overlapping deployment skills and one stale tool wrapper.

## Mechanism

For merge, name target and transferable section; for retire, identify defect and replacement; for improve, specify the section and required change.

## Bad example

```text
Verdicts: “old,” “too long,” “overlaps,” with no path or consequence.
```

## Good example

```text
Merge wrapper A into deployment B’s rollback section; retire stale command C because its verified replacement is D; cite the checked paths and missing mechanism.
```

## Why the change matters

A subsequent implementer can act without reconstructing the audit’s intention.

## Observable expectation

Each disposition has a concrete target, change and evidential reason.

## Limits

Usage frequency alone is not quality; cached unchanged verdicts must preserve their original evidence.

> Independent teaching constructions, not observed model outputs or measured performance.

## Use case

Describe a bulk-email rollout.

## Mechanism

Show before/after evidence, name one-way effects and identify affected callers or users.

## Bad example

```text
Tests green; rollback is one commit.
```

## Good example

```text
Show the dry-run before/after count; state emails cannot be unsent and list the recipient scope plus rollout guard.
```

## Why the change matters

The contrast changes the decision procedure and makes its result inspectable.

## Observable expectation

The review distinguishes code rollback from irreversible external effects.

## Limits

An author risk classification is a claim for review, not independent proof.

> Independent teaching constructions, not observed model outputs or measured performance.

## Use case

Design a file-import interface.

## Mechanism

Give each independent designer a different optimization objective, a shared vocabulary and the same required output schema.

## Bad example

```text
Ask three agents to design the best interface.
```

## Good example

```text
Ask one to minimize entry points, one to support extensions, one to optimize the common caller; compare invariants, usage and hidden complexity.
```

## Why the change matters

The contrast changes the decision procedure and makes its result inspectable.

## Observable expectation

Alternatives expose concrete tradeoffs rather than repeated first ideas.

## Limits

Independent prompts do not guarantee independent ideas; compare actual differences.

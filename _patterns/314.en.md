> Independent teaching constructions, not observed model outputs or measured performance.

## Use case

Resolve a partner retention policy unknown to the developer.

## Mechanism

Interview recipient/context and required return, map every required fact to one fillable question ordered by decision importance.

## Bad example

```text
Grill the developer until they guess the partner policy.
```

## Good example

```text
Ask who owns partner policy and which retention decision is blocked; draft focused questions for that person with uncertainty explicitly allowed.
```

## Why the change matters

The contrast changes the decision procedure and makes its result inspectable.

## Observable expectation

The missing facts are routed to a person able to supply them.

## Limits

One recipient may not own all answers; split by actual knowledge ownership.

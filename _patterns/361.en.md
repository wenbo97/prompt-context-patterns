> Independent teaching constructions, not observed model outputs or measured performance.

## Use case

A release gate compares two routing prompts across three runs of the same fixture.

## Mechanism

Record individual results and report at-least-one success separately from all-trials success, keeping task and budget fixed.

## Bad example

```text
Results pass, fail, fail; claim “three-run reliable” because one run succeeded.
```

## Good example

```text
Record [pass, fail, fail]: success within three attempts=true; all three trials successful=false; retain costs and failures.
```

## Why the change matters

The two measures answer different operational questions and cannot substitute for each other.

## Observable expectation

The raw sequence reproduces both reported outcomes.

## Limits

Do not label a three-run observation as a population probability or infer universal reliability from a small sample.

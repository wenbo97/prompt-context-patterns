> Independent teaching constructions, not observed model outputs or measured performance.

## Use case

An audit scores a service95 but finds a required authorization check absent.

## Mechanism

Define blocker conditions in advance; retain the score while assigning blocked readiness when any applicable blocker is present.

## Bad example

```text
Average good documentation and performance into95 and approve despite the authorization gap.
```

## Good example

```text
Report score95, readiness=blocked, authorization finding and its exact repair/verification gate.
```

## Why the change matters

A high average cannot compensate for a requirement whose failure independently prevents release.

## Observable expectation

The report preserves both the aggregate score and the blocking evidence.

## Limits

Use project-owned blocker policy; do not invent universal thresholds or let a model score replace a real security test.

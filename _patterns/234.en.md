> Independent teaching constructions, not observed model outputs or measured performance.

## Use case

Clean up a job directory named in a process argument.

## Mechanism

Resolve symlinks, require a strict descendant of an allowed root and prior authenticated ownership evidence; refuse without broader fallback and address check/use races.

## Bad example

```text
Trust the OS-delivered path shape and delete it or fall back to the parent root.
```

## Good example

```text
Resolve the candidate under the job root, verify it is below that root and owned by the authorized job, then operate under an immutable hierarchy or safe descriptor.
```

## Why the change matters

The procedure preserves the stated identity or evidence boundary instead of allowing a plausible shortcut to stand in for it.

## Observable expectation

Misderived or attacker-written target data does not enlarge cleanup scope.

## Limits

A writable marker is self-attestation; protect its integrity and bind expected owner to authenticated state.

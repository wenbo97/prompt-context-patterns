> These examples and illustrative results are independently authored teaching materials, not measured model results.

## Use case

Read two permitted logs then summarize. Teaching readLog returns ok/text/path or ok=false/error, and transport can reject. A fulfilled Promise is not necessarily successful business reading.

## Mechanism

Compose documented APIs, parallelize independent reads and inspect every settled transport/business result. Preserve observed text/provenance and failures separately; summarize actual data only and sequence dependent actions afterward.

## Bad example

```text
Invent convenience APIs, lose remaining results on Promise.all rejection or treat fulfilled error objects as log content.
```
{: .example--bad}

## Good example

```text
Use verified teaching readLog(path) on logs/a.log/b.log with Promise.allSettled. Inspect rejection and fulfilled ok=false; send successful text/path to synthesis while listing failures/unread scope. Execute dependencies after inspection without invented data or assuming readLog exists on the real host.
```
{: .example--good}

## Why the change matters

Executable flow makes waits/branches/errors inspectable. Business-state checks prevent returned objects from becoming false success while independent valid data remains useful.

## Observable expectation

A success/B rejection retains A plus B transport failure; fulfilled ok=false is a read failure, not content. Both requested paths have dispositions.

Inspect no hidden errors/unread summaries and verified API/scope. No real logs are read.

## Limits

Envelope/error shapes vary and need documentation. REPL permission/lifetime/output limits remain; composition authorizes no concurrent writes. Provenance is unconfirmed.

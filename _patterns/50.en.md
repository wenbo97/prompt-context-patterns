> Independent teaching constructions, not observed model outputs or measured performance.

## Use case

Assess a reported race in a shared counter.

## Mechanism

Roles make the disputed assumption visible and independently checked.

## Bad example

```text
Have three reviewers vote; majority decides whether the race exists.
```

## Good example

```text
Finder names the interleaving and affected lines. Rebutter checks locks and supplies counterevidence. Adjudicator verifies both against the same revision; keep a race only with a feasible interleaving, otherwise record uncertainty.
```

## Why the change matters

Roles make the disputed assumption visible and independently checked.

## Observable expectation

The verdict cites an interleaving or lock evidence, not vote count.

## Limits

Do not require a defect to exist or treat a legal metaphor as authority.

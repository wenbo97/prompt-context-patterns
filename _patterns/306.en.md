> Independent teaching constructions, not observed model outputs or measured performance.

## Use case

Choose the next lesson after explaining transaction isolation.

## Mechanism

Record demonstrated use, explicit prior-knowledge depth or corrected misconceptions; supersede contrary records instead of erasing history.

## Bad example

```text
Mark isolation mastered because the lesson was displayed.
```

## Good example

```text
Record which anomaly the learner correctly predicts; note the untested cases; supersede that record if later answers show a misconception.
```

## Why the change matters

The contrast changes the decision procedure and makes its result inspectable.

## Observable expectation

Lesson selection can inspect evidence and its limits.

## Limits

Self-reported prior knowledge is a claim with stated depth, not a demonstrated assessment.

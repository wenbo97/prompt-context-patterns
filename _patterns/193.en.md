> Independent teaching constructions, not observed model outputs or measured performance.

## Use case

Audit every catalog entry, including merged and removed items.

## Mechanism

A predicate prevents premature success while an inventory bounds what must be checked.

## Bad example

```text
Finish when the blog looks improved and there are enough examples.
```

## Good example

```text
Done means every original ID has one active/merged/removed decision; every active entry has bilingual fields; references validate; no unreviewed entry remains. Run the coverage checker and list any excluded pages explicitly.
```

## Why the change matters

A predicate prevents premature success while an inventory bounds what must be checked.

## Observable expectation

The final report includes zero unreviewed IDs and the validator result.

## Limits

An exhaustive inventory is limited to the declared corpus; do not imply worldwide completeness.

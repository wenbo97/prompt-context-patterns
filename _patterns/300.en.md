> These examples and illustrative results are independently authored teaching materials, not measured model results.

## Use case

Implement a supplied landing-page reference. The teaching sample visibly says Clear task handoffs and View examples, with text left/comparison right, larger headline and generous space. It shows no hover/submission/mobile behavior. Inspect before reaching for a familiar card template.

## Mechanism

Record readable text, hierarchy, proportions, alignment, spacing, colors and component relationships, separating observations/inferences/unknowns. Implement confirmed relationships; derive hidden behavior from other maintained evidence or needed decisions. Render and compare at matching size. New generated proposals do not replace original-reference evidence.

## Bad example

```text
Glance and build three familiar cards, guess submission/hover and a precise font identity, ignoring the visible wording.
```
{: .example--bad}

## Good example

```text
Extract the teaching observations: exact headline/CTA, two-column text/comparison relationship, larger title and empty space. Font identity, hover and mobile remain unknown pending evidence/decisions. Implement confirmed relationships and compare rendered text/hierarchy/alignment. Use clearer original references for uncertain details, not regenerated images masquerading as original evidence.
```
{: .example--good}

## Why the change matters

Named observations make deviations assessable instead of vaguely similar. Explicit unknowns prevent unseen interactions from becoming supposed design requirements.

## Observable expectation

Compare title, CTA, columns, relative type and space. Missing words, three cards or reversed emphasis should fail.

Unseen hover cannot be reported matched; exact fonts/dimensions need evidence or documented choices. No real landing page is implemented.

## Limits

Raster establishes observed pixels, not semantic HTML, hidden states or exact fonts. Business/accessibility still constrain implementation. Source regeneration supports exploration, not guaranteed preservation of an existing reference.

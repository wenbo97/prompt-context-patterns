> Independent teaching constructions, not observed model outputs or measured performance.

## Use case

An operator approves a supplier reply while an agent concurrently shortens the draft.

## Mechanism

Hash the draft, rotate its revision on edits, and transactionally bind an authorized decision to immutable text and destination.

## Bad example

```text
Use yesterday’s approval flag to send the new shorter draft.
```

## Good example

```text
Draft v2 gets a new hash and epoch; the v1 decision is stale, and transport can use only the approved snapshot’s exact text and destination.
```

## Why the change matters

Approval follows the reviewed artifact instead of a mutable document identity.

## Observable expectation

A stale-epoch or changed-text fixture is refused before dispatch.

## Limits

Hashes prove binding, not operator authority; the owning decision writer must authenticate approval.

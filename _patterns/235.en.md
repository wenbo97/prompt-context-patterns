> Independent teaching constructions, not observed model outputs or measured performance.

## Use case

Validate required sections of a submitted skill.

## Mechanism

Keep reviewed exemption policy outside the unit, require an owned reason and use exact own-membership checks rather than inherited object keys.

## Bad example

```text
Let the skill declare exempt: sections or qualify through a constructor prototype key.
```

## Good example

```text
Consult the validator-owned reviewed exemption list; reject a self-declared exemption and require a documented policy change for a real exception.
```

## Why the change matters

The procedure preserves the stated identity or evidence boundary instead of allowing a plausible shortcut to stand in for it.

## Observable expectation

The input cannot silently authorize its own bypass.

## Limits

The policy repository must itself be review-protected; moving a list alone is not an authorization boundary.

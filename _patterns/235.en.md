> These examples and illustrative results are independently authored teaching materials, not measured model results.

## Use case

A submitted skill omits verification but sets exempt=true. A teaching validator can also accidentally treat inherited constructor as an exemption.

## Mechanism

Keep exemptions in trusted policy outside the tested unit with exact IDs, reason, scope, owner and review expiry. Use own-member checks/Map rather than input fields/prototype keys. Review policy changes under actual authority and record revision; otherwise apply original rules. If targets can alter policy, independence is absent.

## Bad example

```text
Self-declared exempt skips sections, and any object membership including inherited keys grants exceptions.
```
{: .example--bad}

## Good example

```text
Reject self-exemption and consult a reviewed validator-owned exact list. constructor/toString inheritance cannot qualify. Real exceptions record reason/authority/version; missing sections fail unless their precise scope is approved.
```
{: .example--good}

## Why the change matters

Self-granted bypasses nullify checks. External policy and exact membership separate exemption authority from submitted content.

## Observable expectation

Teaching exempt flags, inherited keys and unknown IDs do not bypass. Listed identities exempt only specified sections. Record actual policy version and disclose target-writable lists as failed boundaries.

## Limits

Another file alone is not protection; policy repositories need control. Exceptions are scoped/reviewable, not permanent truth. Frozen lists grant no exemption to this blog/task.

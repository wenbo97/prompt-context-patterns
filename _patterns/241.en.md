> Independent teaching constructions, not observed model outputs or measured performance.

## Use case

Review a release for code and security concerns.

## Mechanism

Distinguish reusable process, reviewer perspective and user-facing command; choose direct invocation for one lens and flat fan-out plus main merge for independent lenses.

## Bad example

```text
Add coordinator personas that delegate through several paraphrasing layers.
```

## Good example

```text
Have the release entry point dispatch two independent leaf reports and reconcile them directly against the release contract.
```

## Why the change matters

The procedure preserves the stated identity or evidence boundary instead of allowing a plausible shortcut to stand in for it.

## Observable expectation

Routing depth and decision ownership are explicit.

## Limits

Flat independent reports fit known-artifact verdicts; shared hypothesis investigation may require interaction instead.

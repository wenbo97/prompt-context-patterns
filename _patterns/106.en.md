> Independent teaching constructions, not observed model outputs or measured performance.

## Use case

Coauthor a migration guide with unresolved rollout details.

## Mechanism

Section boundaries localize feedback and keep missing facts out of finished prose.

## Bad example

```text
Write the full migration guide, invent missing rollout steps, then ask for review.
```

## Good example

```text
Agree on the outline. Draft the compatibility section from supplied facts, review it with the user, and record corrections. Keep rollout unresolved until its owner supplies the policy; then draft that section.
```

## Why the change matters

Section boundaries localize feedback and keep missing facts out of finished prose.

## Observable expectation

Each accepted section uses confirmed facts and exposes its open inputs.

## Limits

Use a full draft when requirements are settled; section-by-section review is not mandatory overhead.

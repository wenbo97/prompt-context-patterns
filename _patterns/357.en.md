> Independent teaching constructions, not observed model outputs or measured performance.

## Use case

Compare two prompt revisions on fixed seeds1 and2: candidate scores0.9/0.5, baseline0.8/0.6.

## Mechanism

Require unique explicit trial IDs, evaluate both revisions for every trial under the same conditions, preserve missing/error outcomes, and compute paired mean difference and win count using the full panel.

## Bad example

```text
Compare candidate’s best0.9 with baseline’s lowest0.6 and claim a0.3 improvement from the requested two-trial panel.
```

## Good example

```text
Retain both pairs:0.9 versus0.8 and0.5 versus0.6; report mean difference0 and win count1/2, so a predeclared positive-mean gate does not pass.
```

## Why the change matters

Matching conditions reduces the confounding from trial choice, and preserving the whole panel prevents cherry-picking one favorable comparison.

## Observable expectation

The report names both immutable revisions, unique trial IDs, per-pair scores, missing/error states and the gate’s aggregate counts.

## Limits

Numbers are a teaching fixture. Two trials do not establish statistical significance or deployment benefit; same seed is insufficient without the same task/data/evaluator conditions, and recorded scores are assertions until their provenance is verified.

> Independent teaching constructions, not observed model outputs or measured performance.

## Use case

Interview about a notification system.

## Mechanism

Treat answers and factual explorations as prerequisite nodes; unresolved nodes block only their dependents.

## Bad example

```text
Ask retention policy, database type and purge schedule all at once.
```

## Good example

```text
First settle legal retention and delivery needs; research provider limits independently; ask purge schedule only after retention is answered.
```

## Why the change matters

The contrast changes the decision procedure and makes its result inspectable.

## Observable expectation

Later questions do not silently assume answers still pending.

## Limits

A question tree can miss a branch; the empty frontier needs explicit scope review.

> Independent teaching constructions, not observed model outputs or measured performance.

## Use case

A reviewer proposes replacing a real dependency with a mock.

## Mechanism

A response commits to a verified change or an evidenced disagreement.

## Bad example

```text
Great point! I will replace it immediately.
```

## Good example

```text
Check what behavior the integration test protects. If the mock would skip migration behavior, explain that with the test path. If the suggestion preserves the required behavior, apply it and show the covering result.
```

## Why the change matters

A response commits to a verified change or an evidenced disagreement.

## Observable expectation

The final response states what was checked and why the suggestion was accepted or declined.

## Limits

Maintain courteous communication; banning words alone does not establish technical correctness.

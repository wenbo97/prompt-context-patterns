> Independent teaching constructions, not observed model outputs or measured performance.

## Use case

A prompt candidate is evaluated a week after the baseline, after the serving environment changed.

## Mechanism

Record the environment and artifact fingerprints; rerun the unchanged control under current conditions, rebuilding any stochastic intermediate artifact, then compare the candidate in that same setting.

## Bad example

```text
Attribute the difference from last week’s baseline entirely to the prompt edit.
```

## Good example

```text
Run the unchanged prompt and candidate in the current environment on the same case design; retain the old score as historical evidence.
```

## Why the change matters

Environmental movement is not silently attributed to the candidate change.

## Observable expectation

Fingerprints, no-change runs and contemporaneous comparisons are recorded.

## Limits

A fingerprint cannot capture every hidden provider change. Controls need comparable cache/state conditions and appropriate replication; they do not alone prove causation.

> These examples and illustrative results are independently authored teaching materials, not measured model results.

## Use case

Deliver an editable order workbook with quantity A2=10 and price B2=15. Total must change with inputs. A literal 150 in C2 looks correct but stays stale when the user changes quantity to 12.

## Mechanism

Identify input and computed cells while preserving existing file conventions. Write =A2*B2 in C2 and briefly label editable inputs and units. Do not save a cached-values-only load that discards formulas. Recalculate with the target engine, then inspect formula strings and results separately. Change quantity in a verification copy to test dependencies while preserving delivery inputs.

## Bad example

```text
Create an editable order with 10 in A2,15 in B2 and 150 in C2. Make it attractive and deliver.
```
{: .example--bad}

## Good example

```text
Create the same editable order with A2=10, B2=15 and C2 formula =A2*B2. Label quantity and price as inputs. Recalculate with the target engine and verify 150. In a copy, change A2 to 12 and confirm 180 with the formula retained; restore original inputs in the final artifact.
```
{: .example--good}

## Why the change matters

Editable artifacts carry relationships between inputs and results, not just current values. Formulas preserve those relationships; real recalculation and input perturbation expose apparently correct hardcoded outputs.

## Observable expectation

Record both formula =A2*B2 and cached result 150. In the verification copy, A2=12 yields 180. Reopen the final file to confirm the formula remains and original quantity 10 was not changed by verification. An empty cache does not alone imply a formula error; check whether calculation ran.

## Limits

Immutable snapshots may legitimately contain values; formulas suit updating models. Verify engine compatibility, calculation mode, external links and macros under the file contract. Error-free formulas do not establish correct business logic, and reading cached values alone does not prove editability.

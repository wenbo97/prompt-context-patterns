> Independent teaching constructions, not observed model outputs or measured performance.

## Use case

Build an editable order workbook with quantity, price and total.

## Mechanism

A formula encodes the relationship that a hardcoded result loses.

## Bad example

```text
A2=10; B2=15; C2=150. Ship it as an editable total.
```

## Good example

```text
A2=10; B2=15; C2=SUM(A2*B2). Recalculate with the target engine. Change A2 to 12 in a verification copy and confirm C2 becomes 180.
```

## Why the change matters

A formula encodes the relationship that a hardcoded result loses.

## Observable expectation

Changing quantity changes the computed total without rewriting the result.

## Limits

Use formulas for editable models; a requested immutable snapshot can legitimately contain values.

> Independent teaching constructions, not observed model outputs or measured performance.

## Use case

A streamed evaluation call keeps sending heartbeat events without finishing.

## Mechanism

Start a non-resetting wall-clock budget for the case; classify expiry as a timeout, release local capacity, cap retries, and retain the underlying-operation identity.

## Bad example

```text
Reset the only timeout after each heartbeat and wait forever.
```

## Good example

```text
Stop local waiting after the case deadline despite fresh heartbeats; record the timeout and handle any still-running request separately.
```

## Why the change matters

Liveness of a connection is separated from completion of the task.

## Observable expectation

The elapsed deadline, released slot and timeout record are observable.

## Limits

Stopping local waiting does not necessarily cancel the remote request or its side effects. Budget and retry policy depend on the task and service.

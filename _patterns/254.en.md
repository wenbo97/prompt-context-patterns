> These examples and illustrative results are independently authored teaching materials, not measured model results.

## Use case

A stream emits keepalives without completion. Teaching case starts UTC10:00 with absolute 10:02 deadline plus separate inactivity timer. Resetting the only timeout permits indefinite occupancy.

## Mechanism

Use nonresetting total deadline and separate liveness. On expiry end local wait, record timeout/attempt and release capacity. Pass deadline before retries/judging; retain underlying request identity and separately verify cancellation/remote status. Race completion does not abort remote work.

## Bad example

```text
Reset two minutes for every heartbeat, retry after timeout and call the remote job cancelled.
```
{: .example--bad}

## Good example

```text
Keep teaching 10:02 fixed. At expiry record timeout, not business zero, release local slot and issue no retry/judge past the shared deadline. Preserve request/possible-remote-running state and use actual cancellation/reconciliation without Promise.race cancellation claims.
```
{: .example--good}

## Why the change matters

Traffic establishes activity rather than completion. Deadlines bound local resources and prevent abandoned retry chains continuing calls.

## Observable expectation

Heartbeat at 10:01 with no terminal by 10:02 times out; no later retry/judge. Inspect local release versus remote unknown and actual timing/identity.

No streaming service runs.

## Limits

Deadlines/recovery are task-specific. Local capacity release does not release remote resources/cost/effects; frozen SDK/runner examples promise no current cancellation.

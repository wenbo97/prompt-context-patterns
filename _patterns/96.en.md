> Independent teaching constructions, not observed model outputs or measured performance.

## Use case

Update a shared library and several dependent callers.

## Mechanism

Batch boundaries localize failure and make incomplete migration explicit.

## Bad example

```text
Change all consumers, retry the full build forever, and skip failures silently.
```

## Good example

```text
Order batches by dependencies and risk. For each, record baseline, changes and covering checks. Stop on repeated error signatures; preserve or roll back only that batch under the approved scope. Do not advance a dependent batch over an unresolved prerequisite.
```

## Why the change matters

Batch boundaries localize failure and make incomplete migration explicit.

## Observable expectation

Previously passing batches stay accounted for and blocked consumers remain visible.

## Limits

Rollback must preserve unrelated edits and cannot repair a dependency by merely skipping it.

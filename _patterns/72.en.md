> Independent teaching constructions, not observed model outputs or measured performance.

## Use case

Two workers share a queue of independent catalog review batches.

## Mechanism

Ownership and readiness prevent duplicate work and premature dependent edits.

## Bad example

```text
Both workers take the first unfinished batch and edit it.
```

## Good example

```text
A task is ready only when its prerequisites pass and acceptance criteria exist. Atomically claim one task with owner and revision. Work only in its assigned files; idle if none are ready, and release the claim with an explicit status.
```

## Why the change matters

Ownership and readiness prevent duplicate work and premature dependent edits.

## Observable expectation

No task has two active owners, and blocked tasks remain unclaimed.

## Limits

A Markdown board needs an external claim protocol when concurrent writers are possible.

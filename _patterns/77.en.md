> These examples and illustrative results are independently authored teaching materials, not measured model results.

## Use case

Initial incident triage has five minutes before handoff. Teaching leads are recent deploys, error logs and database connections; only elevated connections are currently observed, with cause unknown. Bound this phase while preserving useful evidence.

## Mechanism

Define objective, start/cutoff and permitted checks. Prioritize relevant material; record each lead's evidence and confirmed/refuted/inconclusive status. Missing evidence does not refute a lead. At expiry deliver inspected/uninspected scope and the best next check without indefinite extension or a fabricated diagnosis.

## Bad example

```text
Continue examining all possible causes until resolution despite the five-minute handoff. Omit open leads and claim the database cause is established.
```
{: .example--bad}

## Good example

```text
Budget five minutes for this initial phase with recorded start/cutoff. Inspect deploys, logs and connections; attach evidence plus confirmed/refuted/inconclusive status to each lead. At expiry hand off the partial table, unread inputs and best supported next check. Elevated connections are an observation, not a cause. Budget expiry does not resolve the incident.
```
{: .example--good}

## Why the change matters

The budget coordinates effort/handoff timing while a status table preserves uncertainty. Together they prevent both endless investigation and invented completion to meet a deadline.

## Observable expectation

An illustrative handoff says connection elevation observed, retry/leak causes unresolved, next compare calls and connection release. Inspect evidence for attempted leads and real timing rather than an invented within-five-minutes claim.

Missing handoff, evidence-free refutation or cause inferred from elevation fails.

## Limits

Five minutes is a teaching choice; actual objectives/risks may need another budget. Time limits do not establish exhaustive search or change original authority/service-operation rules. The source remains unconfirmed.

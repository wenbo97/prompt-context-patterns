> Independent teaching constructions, not observed model outputs or measured performance.

## Use case

Initial incident triage has five minutes before handoff.

## Mechanism

A budget bounds effort while the status table preserves useful uncertainty.

## Bad example

```text
Investigate every cause until each is fully resolved.
```

## Good example

```text
Budget five minutes for initial triage. For each lead, record evidence and confirmed/refuted/inconclusive status. At expiry, deliver the partial table, missing inputs and next best check; do not pretend the cause is known.
```

## Why the change matters

A budget bounds effort while the status table preserves useful uncertainty.

## Observable expectation

The handoff arrives within budget and accounts for all attempted leads.

## Limits

A time limit is a planning constraint, not proof the investigation is exhaustive.

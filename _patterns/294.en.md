> Independent teaching constructions, not observed model outputs or measured performance.

## Use case

Storyboard cart to payment confirmation.

## Mechanism

For each successor name the preceding action/navigation and the state carried into it.

## Bad example

```text
Show attractive cart, login and receipt screens in arbitrary order.
```

## Good example

```text
Map pay on the populated cart to checkout with the same items, then successful payment to a receipt with the same total.
```

## Why the change matters

The stated contract changes what the agent selects, preserves or verifies, and makes the difference inspectable.

## Observable expectation

Every screen transition has a declared cause and state continuity.

## Limits

Static images illustrate the intended flow; they do not prove implemented behavior.

> Independent teaching constructions, not observed model outputs or measured performance.

## Use case

Audit a web page from source and a Lighthouse report.

## Mechanism

Select mode from available artifacts, label every populated value with its source kind, and leave unmeasured fields explicitly unmeasured.

## Bad example

```text
Invent a real-user p75 from a static scan or relabel the lab result as field data.
```

## Good example

```text
Mark static findings potential impact; show the report value as Lab with its conditions, and keep Field p75 not measured.
```

## Why the change matters

The procedure preserves the stated identity or evidence boundary instead of allowing a plausible shortcut to stand in for it.

## Observable expectation

Numbers retain the meaning and limitations of their source.

## Limits

A source label does not validate the artifact or make different populations/runs comparable. A static or heuristic scanner result is separate from observed live-browser behavior; label the method actually used.

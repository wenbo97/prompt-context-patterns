> Independent teaching constructions, not observed model outputs or measured performance.

## Use case

Write a retry-policy runbook.

## Mechanism

Separate ordered steps from reference, disclose branch-specific reference, and group each concept under one heading.

## Bad example

```text
Put the definition at the top, exceptions in setup notes and the decision table at the bottom.
```

## Good example

```text
Keep retryable definition, eligibility table and non-idempotent exception in one retry-policy reference linked at the retry decision.
```

## Why the change matters

The contrast changes the decision procedure and makes its result inspectable.

## Observable expectation

Reading the decision rule also reveals its exception.

## Limits

Co-location is not duplication; shared authoritative reference can still live in another file.

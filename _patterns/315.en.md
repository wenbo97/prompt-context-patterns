> These examples and illustrative results are independently authored teaching materials, not measured model results.

## Use case

Write a retry runbook where readers need both candidate-error definitions and a non-idempotent-write exception. Teaching rules treat a transient network failure as a candidate, but a write without idempotency guarantees cannot be retried automatically just because of that failure. Scattering these pieces exposes incomplete rules.

## Mechanism

Choose shared-step versus branch-reference tiers, then organize one concept together at the chosen location. The retry decision points to one maintained policy containing definition, eligibility, non-idempotent exception and stop conditions. Shared tasks reference it instead of duplicating versions. Reading the rule should also expose its limits.

## Bad example

```text
Put network-error definitions at the beginning, retry eligibility at the end and non-idempotent restrictions in setup notes. Let a reader retry as soon as a network error matches the table.
```
{: .example--bad}

## Good example

```text
Create one retry-policy section containing candidate definitions, eligibility, the non-idempotent-write exception and stop conditions. Read it at the retry decision. In this teaching contract, a transient failure is a candidate only: establish safe repeatability first, and do not automatically retry writes lacking that guarantee. Keep one maintained reference, not hidden exception copies.
```
{: .example--good}

## Why the change matters

Scattering makes it easier to read a general rule without its exception. Co-location supports decisions based on complete conditions, while information hierarchy still determines entrypoint versus disclosed material.

## Observable expectation

For a teaching non-idempotent write with a transient network failure, the reader sees the exception in the same section and stops automatic retry. A safely repeatable operation still needs eligibility and bound checks.

Inspect reachable maintained references and adjacent caveats, not merely the word idempotent somewhere in the document.

## Limits

Co-location does not mean putting every resource into one file or duplicating shared references. Real eligibility, attempt counts and idempotency follow the service contract. The example is not a validated production retry policy.

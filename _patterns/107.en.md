> Independent teaching constructions, not observed model outputs or measured performance.

## Use case

A deployment runbook may rely on decisions made during drafting.

## Mechanism

Removing drafting history prevents the reviewer from filling gaps with that history.

## Bad example

```text
Ask the author to confirm the runbook is understandable.
```

## Good example

```text
Give a fresh reader only the runbook and expected audience. Ask them to locate the rollback command, explain its trigger and list missing prerequisites. Revise gaps they cannot resolve from the document itself.
```

## Why the change matters

Removing drafting history prevents the reviewer from filling gaps with that history.

## Observable expectation

The reader can locate and explain the required action using document evidence.

## Limits

A fresh model reader may share biases; test important runbooks with real users too.

> Independent teaching constructions, not observed model outputs or measured performance.

## Use case

Scrape 10 URLs and produce a coverage report.

## Mechanism

Inspect each item’s status/error, separate completed/failed/missing items, then retry only appropriate failures.

## Bad example

```text
HTTP 200 means all 10 pages were scraped successfully.
```

## Good example

```text
Read each item; report 8 complete and 2 DNS failures; retain failed URLs for follow-up.
```

## Why the change matters

Transport success and business-operation success are different layers.

## Observable expectation

Completed plus failed/missing items reconcile to the requested inputs.

## Limits

Validate response shape from actual output; do not invent missing results.

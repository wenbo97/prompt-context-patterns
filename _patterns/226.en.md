> Independent teaching constructions, not observed model outputs or measured performance.

## Use case

Design an import-from-URL workflow.

## Mechanism

Name writer, asset and boundary; ask how the intended action can be misused and turn that into a testable failure scenario.

## Bad example

```text
Validate only a successful public HTTPS import.
```

## Good example

```text
Alongside success, test a redirect toward an internal address, oversized content and a caller lacking import rights; choose controls for those declared abuses.
```

## Why the change matters

The procedure preserves the stated identity or evidence boundary instead of allowing a plausible shortcut to stand in for it.

## Observable expectation

Controls are justified by named misuse paths rather than a generic checklist alone.

## Limits

These are scoped test scenarios, not permission to probe arbitrary live systems.

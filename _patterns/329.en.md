> Independent teaching constructions, not observed model outputs or measured performance.

## Use case

Elicit a founder’s product positioning from vague claims such as “we care about quality.”

## Mechanism

Paraphrase the answer, ask a why or concrete-example probe, and close the topic only when repeated probes add no information.

## Bad example

```text
Send twenty abstract questions at once, then synthesize “quality-focused” from one generic answer.
```

## Good example

```text
Ask for one customer incident, paraphrase the consequence, probe why it mattered, and stop after two follow-ups add no new evidence.
```

## Why the change matters

The dialogue deepens an actual answer rather than collecting shallow checklist responses.

## Observable expectation

The notes contain the concrete incident, inferred value and explicit saturation evidence.

## Limits

Saturation is an interview heuristic, not proof the domain is exhaustively known; respect a request to change topic.

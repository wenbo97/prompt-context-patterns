> Independent teaching constructions, not observed model outputs or measured performance.

## Use case

Turn the same outage evidence into an executive decision brief.

## Mechanism

The reader's decision determines which evidence belongs upfront.

## Bad example

```text
Include every log, stack trace and technical detail in the executive brief.
```

## Good example

```text
Audience: executive deciding whether to delay rollout. Lead with observed customer impact, rollout risk and recommendation; attach evidence links. Keep unknown cause explicit and retain technical detail in the linked appendix.
```

## Why the change matters

The reader's decision determines which evidence belongs upfront.

## Observable expectation

The brief supports the rollout decision without hiding uncertainty or losing evidence access.

## Limits

Changing audience never authorizes changing the underlying facts or certainty.

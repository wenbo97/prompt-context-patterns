> Independent teaching constructions, not observed model outputs or measured performance.

## Use case

Prepare a production alert rule that will notify real responders.

## Mechanism

Approval refers to a reviewable action with known effects.

## Bad example

```text
Enable the production alert and ask afterward whether it looks right.
```

## Good example

```text
Prepare the disabled rule and validate its thresholds. Before enabling, show target, recipients and expected notification behavior; obtain approval for enabling this rule. Do not infer consent from silence.
```

## Why the change matters

Approval refers to a reviewable action with known effects.

## Observable expectation

The audit records approval before the enable operation.

## Limits

Honor authorization already granted within its scope; do not repeatedly gate routine work.

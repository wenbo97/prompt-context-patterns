> These examples and illustrative results are independently authored teaching materials, not measured model results.

## Use case

An approved local plan alternates normalizeRow/normalizeRecord while its spec defines the same behavior and only internal callers are affected. Make a reversible naming choice reviewable rather than deriving publish/security permission from adjudication.

## Mechanism

Verify spec/interface/impact and distinguish implementation choice from unsettled product requirements. Record chosen name, evidence, affected callers and rework if wrong; update docs/checks. Preserve rationale in final delivery even after scratch cleanup. Material unknown/out-of-scope decisions follow their own rules.

## Bad example

```text
Choose normalizeRow silently, leave docs inconsistent and delete the only record before delivery.
```
{: .example--bad}

## Good example

```text
Within authorized local scope unify the teaching name as normalizeRow, recording spec basis, synonymous normalizeRecord occurrences and callers. Update docs/verify unchanged behavior. Explain that a different naming decision requires coordinated interface/caller/doc changes, not behavior change. Include rationale/checks in final reporting without deleting its sole evidence or publishing automatically.
```
{: .example--good}

## Why the change matters

Reversible decisions support progress; persistent reasons expose them to correction. Rework scope identifies affected commitments instead of making a choice appear inevitable.

## Observable expectation

An illustrative record contains choice/basis/callers/checks/rework and survives into handoff. Inspect consistent naming and avoid invented duration estimates.

A choice alone without reasons/impact is insufficient.

## Limits

Cost can be rework objects rather than numbers; unknown time stays unknown. This does not authorize unresolved product/security/destructive/external decisions or importing source auto-adjudication beyond current scope.

> Independent teaching constructions, not observed model outputs or measured performance.

## Use case

An approved plan has two inconsistent internal names but the binding spec settles the behavior.

## Mechanism

Resolve within authorized reversible scope using the spec, record the ruling and its rework cost, carry it to affected briefs and expose it in the final report.

## Bad example

```text
Silently choose a name and delete the scratch record before the user can review the decision.
```

## Good example

```text
Record the selected name, spec basis and affected callers; update relevant briefs; include this ruling and its correction cost in the final report.
```

## Why the change matters

The decision remains reviewable after implementation and scratch cleanup.

## Observable expectation

Every recorded ruling appears in the final handoff with its rationale and impact.

## Limits

Do not turn this into permission for security-sensitive, destructive or externally visible actions outside the user’s authorization.

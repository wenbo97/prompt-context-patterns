> Independent teaching constructions, not observed model outputs or measured performance.

## Use case

An agent appears in an external group whose display name resembles an internal operations channel.

## Mechanism

Resolve destination from trusted adapter identity, require scoped participation, classify output for its audience, and recheck the delivery grant at transport.

## Bad example

```text
Treat a channel label “Internal” and a prior bot reply as permission to post debug traces.
```

## Good example

```text
Use the actual external channel ID; a current agent mention permits a scoped business reply, while internal traces stay on the verified operator surface.
```

## Why the change matters

A display name or historical presence cannot supply authentication, consent or send authority.

## Observable expectation

Fixtures cover human-addressed silence, agent-addressed response and changed-destination refusal.

## Limits

Prompt guidance alone is not a transport guard; enforce all send, edit and stream paths in the owning runtime.

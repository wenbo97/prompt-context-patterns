> Independent teaching constructions, not observed model outputs or measured performance.

## Use case

Modernize an existing purple-branded site.

## Mechanism

Declare default, triggering condition and override condition together; carry the selected interpretation through the whole artifact.

## Bad example

```text
Apply the no-purple aesthetic ban and replace the brand color.
```

## Good example

```text
Keep the supplied purple brand as the explicit override; apply restrained contrast and spacing rules around it.
```

## Why the change matters

The stated contract changes what the agent selects, preserves or verifies, and makes the difference inspectable.

## Observable expectation

The output follows the actual brief rather than a universal recipe.

## Limits

Source numeric style dials and subjective bans are unvalidated editorial choices.

> Independent teaching constructions, not observed model outputs or measured performance.

## Use case

Coauthor an article while the author edits it.

## Mechanism

Read current disk contents before each append or targeted rewrite; keep the input corpus separate.

## Bad example

```text
Keep a cached copy and rewrite the entire article after each paragraph.
```

## Good example

```text
Reread the article, append only the agreed paragraph, and apply a requested rewrite to that paragraph without replacing the rest.
```

## Why the change matters

The contrast changes the decision procedure and makes its result inspectable.

## Observable expectation

The author edits survive subsequent agent writes.

## Limits

Rereading is not an atomic concurrency lock; simultaneous writes need stronger coordination.

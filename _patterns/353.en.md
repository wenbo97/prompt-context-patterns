> Independent teaching constructions, not observed model outputs or measured performance.

## Use case

A saved deployment decision predates a rollback and later correction.

## Mechanism

Verify present source state, record observation time, and mark superseded entries explicitly; keep historical reads distinguishable from active recall.

## Bad example

```text
Repeat the old approved rollout because its saved hash is intact.
```

## Good example

```text
Read the current rollback issue, report the old record as historical, and link a corrected handoff that states the rollback and next verification.
```

## Why the change matters

Integrity of bytes does not prove freshness, truth or continuing approval.

## Observable expectation

Normal recall excludes superseded records while historical lookup preserves their status.

## Limits

Links alone do not supersede records; current authorization still comes from the owning system.

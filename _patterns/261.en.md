> Independent teaching constructions, not observed model outputs or measured performance.

## Use case

A form combines rectangles found in PDF points and regions estimated from a raster preview.

## Mechanism

Record page identity, source dimensions, units and origin; convert pixel coordinates to the common page system, then apply the consumer’s origin transform and inspect the result.

## Bad example

```text
Combine pixel and point rectangles in one field list as if their numbers mean the same thing.
```

## Good example

```text
Scale the image rectangle by page/image dimensions, label all rectangles as top-origin points, then convert once for the annotation writer.
```

## Why the change matters

Numeric shape agreement does not imply coordinate meaning agreement.

## Observable expectation

The unit conversions, page mapping and rendered placement can be checked.

## Limits

Rotation, cropping, nonzero page origins and inaccurate detection need additional handling. The source checker compares font points with raw image units and does not validate every geometry invariant.

> Independent teaching constructions, not observed model outputs or measured performance.

## Use case

Build a local feature outline during a requirements discussion.

## Mechanism

Early artifacts give the discussion something concrete without freezing uncertain decisions.

## Bad example

```text
Create all final tracker issues immediately from the first idea.
```

## Good example

```text
Keep a local draft outline. Add a section when its goal is agreed; mark open questions as unresolved. Show the changing draft for feedback. Publish external issues only under the agreed authorization.
```

## Why the change matters

Early artifacts give the discussion something concrete without freezing uncertain decisions.

## Observable expectation

The draft distinguishes agreed sections from open questions.

## Limits

Keep early writes reversible and private; real-time feedback is not a reason to publish prematurely.

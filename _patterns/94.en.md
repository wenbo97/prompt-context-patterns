> Independent teaching constructions, not observed model outputs or measured performance.

## Use case

Identify action items from a supplied meeting transcript.

## Mechanism

Evidence and missing-value handling separate commitments from keyword hits.

## Bad example

```text
Add every sentence containing 'I'll' to the TODO list.
```

## Good example

```text
For each explicit commitment, extract owner, action, due date if stated and transcript location. Mark missing dates unknown. Ignore hypothetical offers and quoted past promises; deduplicate by owner and action before proposing updates.
```

## Why the change matters

Evidence and missing-value handling separate commitments from keyword hits.

## Observable expectation

Every proposed item links a real commitment and no date is invented.

## Limits

Ambiguous implied commitments require confirmation before assignment or external write-back.

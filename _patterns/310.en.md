> Independent teaching constructions, not observed model outputs or measured performance.

## Use case

Prevent forbidden deep imports.

## Mechanism

Inspect existing checks, repair their wiring first, then encode fixed patterns in the cheapest local guardrail.

## Bad example

```text
Add remember not to deep import to the global prompt.
```

## Good example

```text
Wire the existing import checker into CI; add the forbidden-depth rule and verify it rejects a temporary violation.
```

## Why the change matters

The contrast changes the decision procedure and makes its result inspectable.

## Observable expectation

The recurring violation is caught independently of instruction recall.

## Limits

A check must distinguish forbidden from allowed cases; judgment cannot always be reduced to syntax.

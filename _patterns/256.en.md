> Independent teaching constructions, not observed model outputs or measured performance.

## Use case

A resumable extraction evaluation crashes after several completed cases.

## Mechanism

Key durable results by case and repetition; checkpoint completed invocations, record failed attempts separately with class and usage, and resume only missing scorable results.

## Bad example

```text
Write a timeout as a negative answer in the result slot, then skip it forever on resume.
```

## Good example

```text
Record the timeout in the attempt ledger, keep the result slot open, and bind a later valid answer’s grade and trace to that later invocation.
```

## Why the change matters

Infrastructure failure cannot masquerade as a task answer or block honest recovery.

## Observable expectation

Result identities, attempt history and same-call evidence can be reconciled.

## Limits

Retries may still repeat side effects; isolate or make them idempotent. Checkpoint ordering and concurrent writes need implementation-level consistency. Report exclusions and failure rates alongside conditional scores.

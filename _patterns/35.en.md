> Independent teaching constructions, not observed model outputs or measured performance.

## Use case

Route mechanical renaming and concurrency review under a cost budget.

## Mechanism

Selection follows task evidence rather than a universal prestige or price ranking.

## Bad example

```text
Use the cheapest model for all tasks and trust its confident answers.
```

## Good example

```text
Rename task: choose the model that passes the rename fixture. Concurrency review: choose a model that passes the relevant review cases. Set the requested model explicitly only when the host permits it; escalate failed quality checks and record actual usage.
```

## Why the change matters

Selection follows task evidence rather than a universal prestige or price ranking.

## Observable expectation

Cost reports use observed tokens and the chosen model's applicable rates.

## Limits

Model overrides must obey the host and user policy; prices and capability vary.

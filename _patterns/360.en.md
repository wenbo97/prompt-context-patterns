> Independent teaching constructions, not observed model outputs or measured performance.

## Use case

Two stakeholders describe the same feature’s audience differently.

## Mechanism

Capture attributable raw answers first, then synthesize convergences and divergences without editing the source statements.

## Bad example

```text
Replace both answers with one confident “target audience is enterprise” conclusion.
```

## Good example

```text
Raw: A says startups, B says enterprise; Synthesis: audience unresolved, two candidate positions, and a focused alignment question.
```

## Why the change matters

The reader can distinguish what was said from what the agent inferred.

## Observable expectation

Every synthesis claim can be checked against preserved attributable evidence.

## Limits

Avoid storing sensitive verbatim data unnecessarily; raw capture does not turn a statement into truth.

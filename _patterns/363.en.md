> Independent teaching constructions, not observed model outputs or measured performance.

## Use case

A media source changes while a duration probe and checksum are being collected.

## Mechanism

Use a stable snapshot or detect changes across probe/hash operations; key measurements by digest and reject conflicting occurrences.

## Bad example

```text
Hash the old file, measure the replaced file and publish both as one valid receipt.
```

## Good example

```text
Capture/probe/hash one stable snapshot; if available bytes change during collection, reject the bundle and recollect rather than combine versions.
```

## Why the change matters

A receipt is inconsistent when its measurement and identity refer to different content.

## Observable expectation

Repeated digest references agree on duration and every artifact resolves to the recorded size/hash.

## Limits

Integrity does not prove meaning, safe execution or fresh authorization; hash-before/after alone is limited if uncontrolled mutation can evade detection.

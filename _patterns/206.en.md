> Independent teaching constructions, not observed model outputs or measured performance.

## Use case

Choose a durable storage model after comparing two approaches.

## Mechanism

Three gates reserve decision records for choices future maintainers need to understand.

## Bad example

```text
Write an ADR for every renamed variable and every obvious default.
```

## Good example

```text
Write an ADR for choosing append-only storage if changing it later is costly, future readers would not understand the choice, and alternatives had real trade-offs. Record context, alternatives and consequences. Put routine defaults in ordinary documentation.
```

## Why the change matters

Three gates reserve decision records for choices future maintainers need to understand.

## Observable expectation

The ADR explains a meaningful rejected alternative and the chosen consequence.

## Limits

The gates are editorial heuristics; required compliance records may use different criteria.

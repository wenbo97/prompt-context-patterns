> Independent teaching constructions, not observed model outputs or measured performance.

## Use case

A search selects a model, revises its prompt and lowers its reasoning setting in three separate stages.

## Mechanism

Freeze the complete selected tuple and the comparison basis; confirm quality, cost and required behavioral conditions together rather than adding the favorable stage results.

## Bad example

```text
Add each stage’s best saving and quality gain and publish the sum as the final configuration’s performance.
```

## Good example

```text
Run the final model/prompt/setting tuple on the confirmation cases with fixed quality and cost criteria; report those results and any failed criterion.
```

## Why the change matters

Individually selected components can interact and favorable selection runs are not a joint observation.

## Observable expectation

The final configuration identity, predeclared criteria and confirmation records can be checked.

## Limits

Fresh runs of reused selection cases are not an untouched test set. Confirmation requires adequate replication and comparable state; a predicted mechanism does not by itself prove production robustness.

> These examples and illustrative results are independently authored teaching materials, not measured model results.

## Use case

Model selection, prompt revision and lower reasoning each had favorable stages, but teaching M2+P3+E-low never ran jointly. The prompt may depend on richer reasoning.

## Mechanism

Freeze the full tuple, baseline, cases and pricing/usage basis. Predeclare quality tolerance, cost margin and mechanism criteria before fresh joint runs with appropriate repetitions under matched conditions. Verify parameters reach execution stages, retain item failures/usage and judge all gates. Fresh runs on selection cases are reconfirmation, not untouched testing.

## Bad example

```text
Add the best model savings, prompt gains and reasoning savings and call the sum measured joint performance.
```
{: .example--bad}

## Good example

```text
Confirm M2/P3/E-low together under predeclared quality, cost and reduced-duplicate-lookup criteria. Inspect outputs, call counts and usage with configuration identity/failures. Do not add stage-best numbers or label repeated selection cases independent final testing.
```
{: .example--good}

## Why the change matters

Components interact, so stage wins do not guarantee a joint win. Confirmation binds claimed configuration, quality and benefit to one observation set.

## Observable expectation

Teaching lower cost with quality outside tolerance fails; stable quality without predicted lookup reduction leaves mechanism unexplained. Logs must show model, prompt and setting together. No actual prices, paid runs or performance measurements are provided here.

## Limits

Criteria need justification and sample/repetition design is task-specific. Observed mechanisms do not prove production robustness; pricing/cache bases must match. Source fixed gates/counts are workflow choices, not budget authority.

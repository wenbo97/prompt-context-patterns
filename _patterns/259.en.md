> Independent teaching constructions, not observed model outputs or measured performance.

## Use case

A draft expansion plan assumes a 20% conversion rate with no local evidence.

## Mechanism

Answer the task first; point a brief follow-up at the exact assumption, its evidence source and missing context. If verification was requested, perform it inline instead.

## Bad example

```text
End with “Please double-check everything.”
```

## Good example

```text
For this plan, what local data supports the 20% conversion assumption?
```

## Why the change matters

A named dependency gives the user a specific check rather than generic caution.

## Observable expectation

Each prompt identifies a claim, reasoning step or missing datum from the answer.

## Limits

Prompts do not replace required fact checking. A once-per-conversation cap is a source policy, not a validated optimum; executing code does not prove its correctness.

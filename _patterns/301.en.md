> Independent teaching constructions, not observed model outputs or measured performance.

## Use case

Create a retry-policy quiz.

## Mechanism

Make option lengths and formatting comparable, then give feedback based on the tested skill.

## Bad example

```text
Make the correct answer the only long, detailed option.
```

## Good example

```text
Use comparably detailed options, avoid unique emphasis and explain the policy only after the learner chooses.
```

## Why the change matters

The contrast changes the decision procedure and makes its result inspectable.

## Observable expectation

Success is less attributable to option formatting alone.

## Limits

Matching length cannot remove semantic clues or prove valid measurement.

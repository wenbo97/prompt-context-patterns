> Independent teaching constructions, not observed model outputs or measured performance.

## Use case

A browser probe captures an error-shaped console message, but no uncaught-exception evidence.

## Mechanism

Separate observed output, expected contract, confirmation and hypothesis. Derive one conservative finding statement from the observation, repeat its qualifications in summaries, and propose tests on the channel actually observed.

## Bad example

```text
The console printed Error: quota. Headline: the app crashes. Add an exception-only test; mention that the cause was not checked in a footnote.
```

## Good example

```text
Observed: the console logged Error: quota once. No uncaught exception or crash was observed. Use that same bounded claim in the headline, summary and conclusion. A proposed regression must capture the console-message channel; keep the crash hypothesis separate and unconfirmed until a suitable probe establishes it.
```

## Why the change matters

A qualified detail cannot excuse a stronger headline, and an assertion on another channel may miss the original behavior.

## Observable expectation

Every report section retains the observed-once/console-only limits, and the proposed assertion detects that message.

## Limits

This checks claim consistency, not causation. Logging can still accompany a real crash; more evidence may justify a stronger later claim.

> Independent teaching constructions, not observed model outputs or measured performance.

## Use case

A bilingual assistant passes95 of100 examples but fails all five Chinese examples.

## Mechanism

Declare the relevant language slices and acceptance policy, retain each denominator and uncertainty, and block promotion when a required slice fails.

## Bad example

```text
Report95% overall and approve the bilingual feature.
```

## Good example

```text
Report95/100 overall and0/5 Chinese; keep bilingual readiness blocked and add the Chinese failure cluster to the next experiment.
```

## Why the change matters

A broad average can hide concentrated failure in a promised capability.

## Observable expectation

The report contains aggregate and slice counts, predetermined gates and explicit known coverage gaps.

## Limits

Small slices have high uncertainty; do not invent universal cutoffs or repeatedly tune on a held-out test set.

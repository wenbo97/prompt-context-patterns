> Independent teaching constructions, not observed model outputs or measured performance.

## Use case

A dashboard score rises after the failing linter is omitted from the next run.

## Mechanism

Record executed and unavailable checks. Compare like-for-like check sets and unchanged measurement definitions; report changed coverage separately rather than interpreting the aggregate delta as improvement.

## Bad example

```text
Yesterday scored 6 and today scored 9. Say code quality improved by 3 points; ignore that today skipped lint.
```

## Good example

```text
Compare yesterday and today with their check sets and scoring definitions. Today omitted lint, so mark coverage changed and do not claim a composite improvement. Show comparable per-check results and the reason lint was unavailable.
```

## Why the change matters

Removing a difficult measurement cannot manufacture an apparent quality gain.

## Observable expectation

The report states that the aggregate scores are not comparable and identifies the omitted check.

## Limits

Identical check names alone are insufficient when fixtures, tool versions or thresholds changed. Composite weights remain a local heuristic, not a validated quality metric.

> Independent teaching constructions, not observed model outputs or measured performance.

## Use case

Grade five bug-fix expectations.

## Mechanism

Require one unique result per canonical expectation ID, retain canonical text and derive counts; reject missing/duplicate IDs or inconsistent integer summaries.

## Bad example

```text
Accept a fluent report with four results and a summary claiming five passed.
```

## Good example

```text
Require IDs1–5 exactly once; compute passed/failed from their booleans; reject a missing result before reporting success.
```

## Why the change matters

The comparison measures the intended behavioral distinction and prevents a structurally plausible but irrelevant pass.

## Observable expectation

Coverage and arithmetic cannot be invented by the grader summary.

## Limits

Identity/schema checks do not establish the truth of the grader evidence or semantic judgment.

> Independent teaching constructions, not observed model outputs or measured performance.

## Use case

Reorganize 40 invoices into vendor directories.

## Mechanism

Prepare a mapping, preserve original data, resolve conflicts and record each completed move/copy.

## Bad example

```text
Move and rename everything without a record of original paths.
```

## Good example

```text
Copy the invoices to agreed destinations; record original path, destination and checksum; retain a review list for ambiguous files.
```

## Why the change matters

A transformation becomes inspectable and recoverable.

## Observable expectation

Every processed file has a recorded origin/destination and recoverable original.

## Limits

Undo is only valid while destinations remain unchanged; destructive deletion needs its own authorization.

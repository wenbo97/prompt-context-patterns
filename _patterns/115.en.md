> Independent teaching constructions, not observed model outputs or measured performance.

## Use case

Generate slides with a long title and an image caption.

## Mechanism

Rendering exposes layout defects that structural inspection may miss.

## Bad example

```text
Inspect coordinates in slide XML and claim there is no overflow.
```

## Good example

```text
Render every slide at the delivery size. Inspect title clipping, caption overlap, contrast and reading order; fix visible defects and re-render changed slides. Record which renderer and views were checked.
```

## Why the change matters

Rendering exposes layout defects that structural inspection may miss.

## Observable expectation

The delivered version matches the inspected render and has no observed clipping.

## Limits

Visual QA complements structural and accessibility checks; one renderer does not prove every viewer.

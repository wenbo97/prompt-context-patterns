> Independent teaching constructions, not observed model outputs or measured performance.

## Use case

Dispatch a reviewer for a plan containing duplicated logic.

## Mechanism

A neutral brief prevents the coordinator from grading its own implementation through dispatch cues.

## Bad example

```text
Review this diff, but do not flag the duplication because the plan chose it.
```

## Good example

```text
Review the fixed diff against requirements and quality rubric. Here are the plan and report paths. Treat justifications as claims; return evidence-based findings with actual severity, including plan-mandated defects.
```

## Why the change matters

A neutral brief prevents the coordinator from grading its own implementation through dispatch cues.

## Observable expectation

The reviewer may report any in-scope supported finding.

## Limits

State genuine constraints and facts; neutrality does not mean hiding known evidence from reviewers.

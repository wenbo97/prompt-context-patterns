> Independent teaching constructions, not observed model outputs or measured performance.

## Use case

Stop a capture task whose exported artifact ID arrives after the capture process stops.

## Mechanism

Stop the producer, wait for the current run’s terminal export or explicit failure within a bounded deadline, record the outcome, then stop the event listener.

## Bad example

```text
Kill the listener first, stop capture, and report completion without ever receiving the final artifact ID.
```

## Good example

```text
Stop capture while retaining the listener; accept only the matching run’s exported event, record its artifact ID, then clean up the listener.
```

## Why the change matters

Producer shutdown may initiate finalization; removing the observer too early loses the evidence needed to distinguish stopped from completed.

## Observable expectation

The report contains a current-run terminal outcome, or an explicit unresolved timeout; observer cleanup follows that decision.

## Limits

Validate run identity and stale logs. A receipt does not prove artifact quality or persistent availability; disconnected listeners and ambiguous timeout outcomes need reconciliation, not blind retries.

> Independent teaching constructions, not observed model outputs or measured performance.

## Use case

Draft a rollout plan with rollback and ownership requirements.

## Mechanism

Named checks target omissions rather than asking the author to praise its own draft.

## Bad example

```text
Review your rollout plan and make sure it is excellent.
```

## Good example

```text
Before showing the draft, inspect rollback trigger, rollback command and owner. If an owner is absent, mark it unresolved; if the command is untested, say so. Repair contradictions and report remaining gaps.
```

## Why the change matters

Named checks target omissions rather than asking the author to praise its own draft.

## Observable expectation

The published draft contains each required item or a visible gap.

## Limits

Self-review is not independent verification; do not fabricate weaknesses to meet a quota.

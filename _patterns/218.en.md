> These examples and illustrative results are independently authored teaching materials, not measured model results.

## Use case

Extract titles and prices from 1,000 product pages with standard, sale and out-of-stock layouts. Pilot a contract before batch execution exposes inconsistent fields.

## Mechanism

Define title:string, price:number-or-null, currency and extraction status. Separate missing price from fetch failure rather than using 0. Sample each layout and check readiness, response nesting and per-page status. Manually compare fields with pages, record mismatches and repilot revisions. Scaling retains per-item validation, failures, retries and cost scope.

## Bad example

```text
Extract all 1,000 pages with shifting fields. Set missing price to 0 and treat outer API success as whole-batch success.
```
{: .example--bad}

## Good example

```text
Pilot standard, sale and out-of-stock pages with fixed fields and null/error rules. Missing price is null, fetch failure is error, and sale-price selection must be explicit. Inspect inner status and source per page before scaling in batches with item checks.
```
{: .example--good}

## Why the change matters

A pilot exposes layout/contract mismatches while changes are cheap. Explicit missing/failure states prevent complete-looking but semantically wrong data.

## Observable expectation

Teaching pages yield normal price, the declared sale-price choice and null for absent price. DNS failure must not become 0. Check title/currency/status and reconcile sample counts with accepted/failing records. Without execution, provide a pilot design only.

## Limits

Sample success does not establish population correctness; retain drift monitoring and item checks. Dynamic content, currency, login and blocking affect results. Frozen batching/delay guidance needs actual-contract verification, and paid calls require authorization.

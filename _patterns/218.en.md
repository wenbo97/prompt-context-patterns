> Independent teaching constructions, not observed model outputs or measured performance.

## Use case

Extract title and price from 1,000 product pages.

## Mechanism

Fix schema and null/error rules, test representative pages, inspect results and only scale after the contract is usable.

## Bad example

```text
Change requested fields per page and launch 1,000 extracts immediately.
```

## Good example

```text
Fix title:string and price:number-or-null; test several distinct page layouts; inspect missing fields before scaling.
```

## Why the change matters

The sample exposes contract mismatch before broad execution.

## Observable expectation

Pilot outputs conform to the same schema and failures are recorded.

## Limits

A passing sample does not prove full population correctness; retain per-item validation.

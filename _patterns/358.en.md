> Independent teaching constructions, not observed model outputs or measured performance.

## Use case

Evaluate an incident assistant on the evidence available when an outage began.

## Mechanism

Freeze the decision cutoff, distinguish event time from availability time, and exclude later resolutions or corrected records from the input context.

## Bad example

```text
Retrieve the later postmortem and give its root cause to the assistant while claiming a historical evaluation.
```

## Good example

```text
Build the replay from records available at the cutoff; keep the later postmortem only as the evaluation label, outside model input.
```

## Why the change matters

Future information cannot inflate the apparent ability to decide from earlier evidence.

## Observable expectation

The fixture records cutoff and availability provenance and excludes post-cutoff input records.

## Limits

Current tasks may require the latest facts; this boundary applies to historical evaluation. Unknown availability time is a stated gap, not proof of eligibility.

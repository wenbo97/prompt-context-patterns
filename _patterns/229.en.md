> Independent teaching constructions, not observed model outputs or measured performance.

## Use case

Two workers receive the same publish request.

## Mechanism

Use an atomic uniqueness gate before execution; reject a reused key whose canonical payload differs, and define in-flight duplicate behavior.

## Bad example

```text
Both check not seen, publish, then record the key.
```

## Good example

```text
One unique claim wins before publishing; the duplicate waits or returns pending; a changed article under the same key is rejected.
```

## Why the change matters

The procedure preserves the stated identity or evidence boundary instead of allowing a plausible shortcut to stand in for it.

## Observable expectation

Concurrent delivery does not bypass the same-intent guard.

## Limits

An atomic local claim cannot prove an external effect completed; crash recovery still needs the unknown-outcome protocol.

> Independent teaching constructions, not observed model outputs or measured performance.

## Use case

A large migration cannot choose rollout steps before compatibility research.

## Mechanism

A visible frontier avoids pretending downstream decisions are already knowable.

## Bad example

```text
Invent a detailed rollout sequence now so the plan appears complete.
```

## Good example

```text
Destination: a decision-complete migration specification. Open ticket: verify library compatibility. Future rollout choice stays in not-yet-specified until that result exists; then promote it to a precise decision ticket with dependencies.
```

## Why the change matters

A visible frontier avoids pretending downstream decisions are already knowable.

## Observable expectation

The map distinguishes actionable questions, future unknowns and out-of-scope work.

## Limits

Do not use uncertainty to postpone questions that are already precise and answerable.

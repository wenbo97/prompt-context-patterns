> These examples and illustrative results are independently authored teaching materials, not measured model results.

## Use case

A mail interface's New Email button must clear the selected thread and open the composer. Teaching action selectThread(null) also sets composeOpen=false. Opening before clearing therefore closes the panel again.

## Mechanism

Find the handler and list each call's reads, writes, resets and asynchronous effects in execution order. Compare them with the expected final state: thread=null and composeOpen=true. Under the actual contract, clear first and open second, or use one coherent transition. After related asynchronous work finishes, check state and the visible panel from both selected-thread and empty-thread entry states.

## Bad example

```text
Fix New Email by calling setComposeOpen(true), then selectThread(null). Treat the button as successful once the opening setter runs.
```
{: .example--bad}

## Good example

```text
Audit the complete New Email handler under its action contracts. Since selectThread(null) resets composeOpen, clear the thread before opening. After the whole sequence, assert thread=null, composeOpen=true, a visible composer and expected input focus. Check whether later asynchronous callbacks close it again.
```
{: .example--good}

## Why the change matters

A correct local action does not establish a correct interaction. An action-effect map exposes a later reset overwriting an earlier write. Final state and visible behavior bind verification to what the button promises.

## Observable expectation

The teaching old trace is false→true→false, leaving the composer closed. Clearing then opening ends at true. Test both selected and empty thread states. Injecting a delayed reset should make the regression check fail and identify the later action.

## Limits

Reordering can affect drafts, selection and focus, which also need their product assertions. Async races may require cancellation, version checks or a unified transition; swapping synchronous calls does not fix every case. Source action names are illustrative; inspect the target application's actual implementation.

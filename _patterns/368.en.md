> Independent teaching constructions, not observed model outputs or measured performance.

## Use case

Draft release notes and a newsletter in the same author’s voice from five supplied samples.

## Mechanism

Identify sentence rhythm, claim strength, transition and vocabulary norms; distinguish public/private registers and reuse the confirmed profile.

## Bad example

```text
Ask for “professional LinkedIn style” and rewrite the author into generic platform clichés.
```

## Good example

```text
Derive “short concrete claims, qualified numbers, no bait questions” from the supplied samples; apply that same profile to the release note and newsletter, adding only medium-specific context.
```

## Why the change matters

The adaptation follows source evidence while keeping a single reusable style contract.

## Observable expectation

Both drafts can be compared to cited source features and one profile.

## Limits

Style is contextual; do not treat an author fingerprint as universal policy or persist private samples without authorization.

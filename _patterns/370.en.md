> Independent teaching constructions, not observed model outputs or measured performance.

## Use case

A developer asks to add export functionality, and code happens to cap queries at 100 rows.

## Mechanism

Inspect current technical behavior; obtain product limits from an authoritative brief or user; label unresolved limits as assumptions rather than business facts.

## Bad example

```text
Conclude “free users may export 100 rows” solely because a query uses LIMIT 100.
```

## Good example

```text
Record “query currently fetches 100 rows”; separately note “export entitlement limit unspecified,” and request or document the material product decision before enforcing a new cap.
```

## Why the change matters

Observed code explains current behavior without manufacturing a requirement.

## Observable expectation

The brief has separate discovered facts, supplied product constraints and unresolved assumptions.

## Limits

A product artifact in the repo can be authoritative; this separation does not require asking about facts already supplied.

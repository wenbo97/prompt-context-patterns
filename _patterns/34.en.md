> These examples and illustrative results are independently authored teaching materials, not measured model results.

## Use case

Choose synchronous or queued image processing. Teaching constraints require acknowledgment within 1 second, processing may take several seconds, and accepted work must survive restart; cost limits are unknown. Two planners need equal inputs and independent initial alternatives.

## Mechanism

Within authorized multi-model planning, give both identical requirements, constraints and unknowns without exposing each other’s or the facilitator’s answer. Save drafts before cross-reviewing timeout, restart and duplication failures. Synthesize alternatives, evidence, trade-offs and unknowns without forced consensus. The owner chooses using facts and preferences; external data/costs follow actual authorization.

## Bad example

```text
Show both models the same preferred plan and discuss until they agree on the best architecture. Remove disagreements.
```
{: .example--bad}

## Good example

```text
Give two planners the acknowledgment, durability and unknown-cost constraints independently. Save synchronous/queue proposals before cross-questioning failure cases. Preserve drafts and material disagreement, identify load/cost assumptions needing evidence and present an owner decision rather than a model vote.
```
{: .example--good}

## Why the change matters

Independent drafts reduce direct anchoring on the first answer; cross-review then exposes different failure surfaces. Preserved disagreement gives the final choice reviewable reasons instead of hidden trade-offs.

## Observable expectation

Teaching review retains both drafts and addresses long processing versus synchronous acknowledgment, queue persistence/retry costs and unknown load. Restart must be considered. Agreement without evidence still leaves a conclusion unverified.

## Limits

Models may share training biases; opinion count is not truth. Separate perspectives without distinct models must be labeled honestly. Frozen invocation details support the mechanism only; this entry does not authorize delegation, external sharing or paid calls.

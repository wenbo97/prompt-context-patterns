> Independent teaching constructions, not observed model outputs or measured performance.

## Use case

Choose between queue-based and synchronous image processing.

## Mechanism

Independent initial plans reduce direct anchoring on the first proposal.

## Bad example

```text
Have two agents discuss until they agree on the best architecture.
```

## Good example

```text
Give two planners the same latency, cost and durability constraints without showing each other's plans. Save both plans; cross-review failure cases. Present trade-offs and unresolved facts for the owner to choose.
```

## Why the change matters

Independent initial plans reduce direct anchoring on the first proposal.

## Observable expectation

Both alternatives and their rejected trade-offs remain inspectable.

## Limits

Two models can share biases; disagreement and agreement both need factual checks.

> Independent teaching constructions, not observed model outputs or measured performance.

## Use case

Plan a tool importing partner data.

## Mechanism

Validate dealbreakers before implementation, important adjustable bets next and secondary optimizations after the core is supported.

## Bad example

```text
Spend the first experiment choosing button colors while assuming the partner will supply a usable feed.
```

## Good example

```text
First test whether the partner can and will provide the required feed; next test the update model; defer cosmetic preferences until that core assumption holds.
```

## Why the change matters

The procedure preserves the stated identity or evidence boundary instead of allowing a plausible shortcut to stand in for it.

## Observable expectation

The first experiment can falsify the direction before costly construction.

## Limits

Assumption importance depends on the actual goal; source market hierarchies and numeric heuristics are not measured efficacy.

> Independent teaching constructions, not observed model outputs or measured performance.

## Use case

Onboarding includes an account request and local environment setup.

## Mechanism

Dependency edges allow independent progress without simulating human decisions.

## Bad example

```text
Complete every step in a fixed order, including answering approvals yourself.
```

## Good example

```text
Graph: account approved → remote login; local setup → local build. Mark account approval human-only and local setup agent-runnable. Persist statuses; do independent setup while awaiting approval, then verify granted access before login.
```

## Why the change matters

Dependency edges allow independent progress without simulating human decisions.

## Observable expectation

Human steps remain pending until real evidence arrives.

## Limits

An old completed record does not prove current credentials or machine state.

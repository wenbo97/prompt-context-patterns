> Independent teaching constructions, not observed model outputs or measured performance.

## Use case

A background dependency scan takes several minutes.

## Mechanism

Status messages distinguish progress from success and preserve gaps.

## Bad example

```text
Run silently and finish with 'done'.
```

## Good example

```text
Before scanning, name the target. After discovery, report 12 manifests found and the current check. Finish with complete, partial or blocked plus tested revision, findings and missing inputs.
```

## Why the change matters

Status messages distinguish progress from success and preserve gaps.

## Observable expectation

Partial coverage cannot be mistaken for a complete scan.

## Limits

Report observed counts, not invented estimates; avoid narrating every internal decision.

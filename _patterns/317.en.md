> Independent teaching constructions, not observed model outputs or measured performance.

## Use case

Explain why a ticket must wait.

## Mechanism

Restate the decision in shared nouns and supply the premise the reader lacks, then trim redundancy.

## Bad example

```text
Blocked by dependency.
```

## Good example

```text
The exporter reads the new schema. That schema is not landed, so starting export now would build against the old format.
```

## Why the change matters

The contrast changes the decision procedure and makes its result inspectable.

## Observable expectation

The reader can explain the dependency rather than receiving only a shorter label.

## Limits

Clarity is audience-dependent; ask which premise remains unclear.

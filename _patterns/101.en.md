> Independent teaching constructions, not observed model outputs or measured performance.

## Use case

Create a poster for a night-time astronomy lecture.

## Mechanism

A concrete concept constrains composition instead of relying on adjectives.

## Bad example

```text
Make the astronomy poster beautiful, artistic and museum quality.
```

## Good example

```text
Concept: quiet observation. Use a dark field, one star-map motif, generous empty space and a clear date/location block. Sketch the hierarchy first, then render the poster and check legibility.
```

## Why the change matters

A concrete concept constrains composition instead of relying on adjectives.

## Observable expectation

The rendered poster visibly follows the hierarchy and keeps event details readable.

## Limits

An aesthetic concept is a creative choice, not evidence of improved quality.

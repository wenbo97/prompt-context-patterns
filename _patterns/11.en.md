> Independent teaching constructions, not observed model outputs or measured performance.

## Use case

Create a bug report from logs containing bearer tokens and email addresses.

## Mechanism

Selection removes sensitive fields before copying them into another artifact.

## Bad example

```text
Paste the full request headers and customer email into the public issue.
```

## Good example

```text
Keep timestamp, route and error code. Replace bearer token with [REDACTED-TOKEN] and email with [REDACTED-EMAIL]. Use obviously fake credentials in fixtures; load real values from approved secret storage.
```

## Why the change matters

Selection removes sensitive fields before copying them into another artifact.

## Observable expectation

The report and fixture contain no literal credentials or personal email.

## Limits

Redaction needs domain-specific detectors and review; a model may miss disguised identifiers.

> Independent teaching constructions, not observed model outputs or measured performance.

## Use case

Audit whether a human requested an external action from a child-agent transcript.

## Mechanism

Establish record meanings and parent/child relationships, then attribute intent only to records proven to be human-typed.

## Bad example

```text
Treat every role=user message inside the child transcript as direct human permission.
```

## Good example

```text
Identify the role=user record as a parent dispatch; locate the actual human request in the parent history before stating authorization.
```

## Why the change matters

A serialized role label does not uniquely identify the originating actor.

## Observable expectation

The report records actor provenance and the source of any claimed human instruction.

## Limits

When the human record is unavailable, report the limitation rather than inventing intent.

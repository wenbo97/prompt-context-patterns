> Independent teaching constructions, not observed model outputs or measured performance.

## Use case

Mock up an analytics homepage.

## Mechanism

Inspect every displayed number; retain sourced values, label samples and remove invented precision presented as fact.

## Bad example

```text
Invent 47.2% growth to make it look real.
```

## Good example

```text
Use the provided growth figure, or display Sample data beside 47.2%; keep unsupported performance claims out of the published copy.
```

## Why the change matters

The stated contract changes what the agent selects, preserves or verifies, and makes the difference inspectable.

## Observable expectation

Realism does not silently convert invented content into evidence.

## Limits

Comments alone may not be visible to the audience; choose disclosure appropriate to the artifact.

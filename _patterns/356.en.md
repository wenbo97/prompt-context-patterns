> Independent teaching constructions, not observed model outputs or measured performance.

## Use case

A worker is waiting on a tool call and an inspection command sent to its own queue cannot run.

## Mechanism

Use a separate observer to read bounded current transcript/status evidence, correlate pending calls with results and record timestamp and coverage; keep observation separate from stopping or controlling the worker.

## Bad example

```text
Queue “show loop status” behind the blocked call and report that monitoring is running even though the command cannot dequeue.
```

## Good example

```text
Inspect the task transcript from a separate terminal, identify an unmatched call with its observed age and report stale or unknown evidence without claiming the observer terminated it.
```

## Why the change matters

A monitor that shares the blocked execution path cannot provide timely independent observation.

## Observable expectation

The inspection returns a bounded snapshot or an explicit access/read failure without waiting for the worker queue to advance.

## Limits

A stale transcript is not proof the process is stuck; validate session identity and freshness. Observation does not grant authority to kill, steer or restart, and missing telemetry cannot be treated as an empty healthy state.

> These examples and illustrative results are independently authored teaching materials, not measured model results.

## Use case

A generation service has 10 teaching budget units left. Two simultaneous requests each estimate 6 units. If the front door only reads balance and workers charge later, both can be admitted. In-flight commitments must be handled at the shared budget boundary.

## Mechanism

Make checking and reservation atomic in the authoritative store. Successful admission creates a reservation ID carried into the queue; failed admission does not enqueue. Use an idempotent request identity to prevent duplicate reservations on retries. Handle reservation success followed by enqueue failure through compensation or a transactional outbox. Reconcile actual usage on completion with explicit failure and expiry release rules.

## Bad example

```text
Let each 6-unit request read a balance of 10 and enqueue. Deduct when workers start; the front-door check will control the budget.
```
{: .example--bad}

## Good example

```text
Atomically check and reserve for these concurrent requests. The first reserves 6 and enqueues with its reservation ID; the second sees 4 available and is denied enqueue. Record request identity and reservation state, compensate enqueue failure under the contract, and reconcile actual worker usage without treating completion as a new charge.
```
{: .example--good}

## Why the change matters

Separate checking and reservation lets concurrent requests decide against the same old balance. Atomic admission counts unfinished commitments; binding jobs to reservations makes retries and compensation traceable.

## Observable expectation

The teaching concurrency test admits one job, reserves 6 and leaves 4 available, with no denied job in the queue. Repeating the same request creates no second reservation. An enqueue failure releases the reservation or leaves a recoverable pending-enqueue state. Actual usage 5 returns 1 and closes the reservation under the chosen contract.

## Limits

Estimates are not invoices. Strict spend caps also require bounded execution, coverage of all billable paths and reliable reconciliation. Do not expire a reservation freely while its worker is still running. Partial failure is especially relevant across separate queue and budget systems. These units are not currency or provider pricing advice.

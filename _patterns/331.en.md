> Independent teaching constructions, not observed model outputs or measured performance.

## Use case

Two simultaneous generation requests each need an estimated6 units, with10 units remaining.

## Mechanism

Check-and-reserve under the authoritative shared budget boundary before enqueue; reconcile the reservation against actual completion.

## Bad example

```text
Both front-end checks see10; both jobs enqueue and charge only when workers start, admitting12.
```

## Good example

```text
The first admission reserves6; the second sees4 and stays blocked. Record the reservation ID with the accepted job.
```

## Why the change matters

The admission decision includes concurrent in-flight commitments instead of an outdated balance.

## Observable expectation

The concurrent fixture admits one job and records one reservation; a denied request is absent from the queue.

## Limits

Estimates are not exact invoices; strict spend caps also need bounded execution, accurate reconciliation and controlled reservation expiry.

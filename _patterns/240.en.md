> Independent teaching constructions, not observed model outputs or measured performance.

## Use case

Hand one skill directory to another agent.

## Mechanism

List mandatory references and resolve them from the containing artifact under the shipped/handed-off layout; package or remap missing dependencies.

## Bad example

```text
Copy the body while leaving its ../../references targets only in the author checkout.
```

## Good example

```text
Include the required references inside the handed-off package and validate their relative links from the received skill before use.
```

## Why the change matters

The procedure preserves the stated identity or evidence boundary instead of allowing a plausible shortcut to stand in for it.

## Observable expectation

A context pointer remains reachable where the work actually runs.

## Limits

Existing-file checks do not prove licensing, contents or current semantics; optional references need explicit fallback.

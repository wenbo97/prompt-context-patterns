> Independent teaching constructions, not observed model outputs or measured performance.

## Use case

A race only occurs across callers, but the available test isolates one caller.

## Mechanism

The architectural gap remains visible instead of being hidden by a passing irrelevant test.

## Bad example

```text
Write the shallow unit test and claim the race is permanently covered.
```

## Good example

```text
Explain why the single-caller seam cannot reproduce the race. Identify the missing shared-state seam, retain the real reproduction evidence and propose the smallest integration harness. Mark regression coverage incomplete until it exercises both callers.
```

## Why the change matters

The architectural gap remains visible instead of being hidden by a passing irrelevant test.

## Observable expectation

The report states exactly which behavior the current suite cannot lock down.

## Limits

Use meaningful available checks too; a missing ideal seam is not an excuse to do no verification.

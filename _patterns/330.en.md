> Independent teaching constructions, not observed model outputs or measured performance.

## Use case

Two required reviewers assess a release note; one flags an unsupported benchmark claim.

## Mechanism

Provide the original spec and exact artifact to isolated reviews, combine required verdicts with AND, repair supported blockers and rereview the changed artifact within a bounded cycle.

## Bad example

```text
Count one PASS as sufficient or combine a PASS on revision1 with another on revision2.
```

## Good example

```text
Keep revision2 blocked until both required reviewers evaluate revision2 with the same rubric and its unsupported claim is resolved.
```

## Why the change matters

The gate does not mix artifact versions or let one required review erase another.

## Observable expectation

The record identifies the artifact/rubric version, each verdict and bounded repair outcomes.

## Limits

Reviewers can share errors or raise false positives; investigate evidence. The gate supplies review evidence, not deployment authorization.

> Independent teaching constructions, not observed model outputs or measured performance.

## Use case

Position a PDF annotation using an axis-aligned screen-to-document mapping with unknown scale and origin.

## Mechanism

Use two distinct coordinate pairs per unknown axis or independently established parameters; solve the transform, test an additional anchor and invalidate it after relevant layout changes.

## Bad example

```text
From document100→screen250 alone, assume scale2.5 and predict document200→screen500.
```

## Good example

```text
Use100→250 and300→650: scale2, origin50; verify an independent document200 anchor at450 before positioning.
```

## Why the change matters

One pair cannot determine both unknown scale and origin, and fitting anchors do not independently validate the fit.

## Observable expectation

The report records parameters, separate check residual and the current layout/zoom identity.

## Limits

The example assumes an unrotated axis-aligned mapping; degenerate axes, unknown tolerance, rotation or changing geometry require a different model or a stop.

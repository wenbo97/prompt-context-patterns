> Independent teaching constructions, not observed model outputs or measured performance.

## Use case

Deliver a redacted failure-analysis archive.

## Mechanism

Audit all exported files for sensitive values, then resolve each finding to included evidence; reconcile files and verify the final archive matches the reviewed set.

## Bad example

```text
Treat a CLEAN privacy result as proof every exported diagnosis remains supported.
```

## Good example

```text
Record privacy CLEAN and evidence limited for a claim whose tool-result body was withheld; state that limitation and archive exactly the reviewed files.
```

## Why the change matters

Removing secrets and preserving proof are different objectives.

## Observable expectation

The handoff states separate privacy and evidence outcomes and names unsupported findings.

## Limits

A privacy audit is not exhaustive certification; edits after review require affected checks again.

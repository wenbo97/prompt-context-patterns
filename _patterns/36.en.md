> Independent teaching constructions, not observed model outputs or measured performance.

## Use case

Hand a reviewer an isolated database-migration check.

## Mechanism

The brief transfers binding context without forwarding abandoned conversation.

## Bad example

```text
Based on everything earlier, review the migration and tell me what you think.
```

## Good example

```text
Task: assess migration 0042.sql at commit A. Table has 50M rows; adding NOT NULL is the concern. Read docs/db-contract.md. Return locking risk, evidence locations and missing facts; do not execute the migration.
```

## Why the change matters

The brief transfers binding context without forwarding abandoned conversation.

## Observable expectation

The reviewer can explain its exact target, constraints and output without parent history.

## Limits

Inspect the cited artifacts; a concise brief can still omit a critical dependency.

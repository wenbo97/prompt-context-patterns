> Independent teaching constructions, not observed model outputs or measured performance.

## Use case

Import 120 leads while intermittent failures are possible.

## Mechanism

Chunk within service limits, record successful item IDs and continuation state, then resume unresolved work only.

## Bad example

```text
Restart the entire import after batch three fails.
```

## Good example

```text
Import bounded chunks; save successful IDs after each chunk; resume remaining IDs after the failure.
```

## Why the change matters

A checkpoint preserves acknowledged work across interruptions.

## Observable expectation

The resume list excludes already acknowledged items and reconciles every requested ID.

## Limits

Checkpointing alone does not ensure exactly-once writes; combine with idempotency keys or lookups.

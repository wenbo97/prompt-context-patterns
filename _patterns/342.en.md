> Independent teaching constructions, not observed model outputs or measured performance.

## Use case

A local input-validation lesson appears in two independent services.

## Mechanism

Compare compatible triggers and actions across projects, retain evidence and scope, and downgrade or withhold promotion on contradictory corrections.

## Bad example

```text
Make a React-only convention global after one successful use.
```

## Good example

```text
Compare matching validation lessons from both services, confirm applicability, and propose a broader rule with the two observations and known exceptions.
```

## Why the change matters

Promotion is based on cross-context recurrence rather than the confidence of one session.

## Observable expectation

The proposal cites the contributing projects, observations and exclusions.

## Limits

Repository confidence thresholds are operational settings, not calibrated probabilities; governed policy still needs its normal review.

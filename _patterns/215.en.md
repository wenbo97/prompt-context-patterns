> Independent teaching constructions, not observed model outputs or measured performance.

## Use case

Calculate engagement across all subscribers.

## Mechanism

Accumulate pages until the documented terminal cursor; record page count, record count and any failure/cap.

## Bad example

```text
Compute all-subscriber engagement from the first 50 returned records.
```

## Good example

```text
Follow meta.next_cursor until null; aggregate records; label partial if fetching fails or a cap is reached.
```

## Why the change matters

A successful page response says nothing about unrequested pages.

## Observable expectation

The report records terminal cursor and page count, or explicitly says partial.

## Limits

Completeness is relative to filter and permissions; never bypass quotas to finish.

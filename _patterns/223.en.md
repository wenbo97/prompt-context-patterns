> Independent teaching constructions, not observed model outputs or measured performance.

## Use case

Read invoices for the user’s subsidiary B while two organizations are connected.

## Mechanism

List connections, match the intended organization and include its tenant_id in subsequent calls.

## Bad example

```text
Omit tenant_id and assume the first connected organization is B.
```

## Good example

```text
List connections; match subsidiary B to tenant TB; read invoices with tenant_id=TB.
```

## Why the change matters

An authenticated account can still address the wrong organization.

## Observable expectation

Every relevant call and report identifies tenant TB.

## Limits

Ask when the user’s organization cannot be uniquely matched; redact credentials.

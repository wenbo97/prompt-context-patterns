> Independent teaching constructions, not observed model outputs or measured performance.

## Use case

Compare a new summarizer with an incumbent whose outputs became the answer key.

## Mechanism

Label reference origin, verify factual criteria independently, and choose rubric or blind comparison when multiple valid outputs exist.

## Bad example

```text
Mark every stylistic departure from the incumbent summary wrong because its text is the gold answer.
```

## Good example

```text
Check both summaries against the source facts and an independently reviewed rubric; retain the incumbent text as a comparison artifact with its origin labeled.
```

## Why the change matters

Imitation of one candidate is separated from correctness on the task.

## Observable expectation

Reference provenance and the independently checked criteria are recorded.

## Limits

Human verification can also be wrong. A rubric or pairwise judge still requires calibration and bias checks.

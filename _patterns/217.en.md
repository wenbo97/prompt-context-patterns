> Independent teaching constructions, not observed model outputs or measured performance.

## Use case

Hand over generated audio for a report that will be read next month.

## Mechanism

Inspect output lifetime, download or persist through an authorized durable destination, verify content and record the durable path.

## Bad example

```text
Give only an expiring presigned URL and call it a permanent archive.
```

## Good example

```text
Save the audio in the agreed durable folder; verify its bytes and provide that path with the original job identity.
```

## Why the change matters

A temporary retrieval token is different from retained content.

## Observable expectation

The artifact remains accessible through a verified durable destination.

## Limits

Respect privacy and retention policy; do not extend retention without task authorization.

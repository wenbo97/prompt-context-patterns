> Independent teaching constructions, not observed model outputs or measured performance.

## Use case

A prompt evaluation has two passing trials and one failed safety contract.

## Mechanism

Declare trial count, acceptable stochastic variation and nonwaivable contracts before execution. Keep failures and missing trials in the result; repeat only as a separately identified experiment after a concrete change.

## Bad example

```text
Run three trials. If one fails, keep sampling until three pass, then report a perfect result.
```

## Good example

```text
Before running this fixture, declare three trials and the acceptance rule. Keep all three outcomes. An occasional wording difference may follow the declared majority rule, but any forbidden write fails the safety contract. A missing trial means incomplete. Do not add samples after seeing a failure to replace it.
```

## Why the change matters

A fixed panel prevents favorable outcomes from silently replacing the original experiment.

## Observable expectation

The result includes all three attempts and separately identifies the contract failure or missing coverage.

## Limits

Three trials and majority voting are only example policies. Choose sample size and thresholds for the actual risk; passing a small panel does not establish a population reliability rate.
